Acceptable Use
Guest Wi-Fi Acceptable Use Policy

By connecting, you agree to use this network in a lawful, appropriate manner consistent with district rules and event expectations.

  • Appropriate content: Access is intended for general web browsing and event-related needs.
  • No illegal activity: Do not use the network to violate any law, regulation, or policy.
  • No harmful behavior: Do not attempt to scan, disrupt, bypass filtering, or access systems without permission.
  • Respect others: No harassment, threats, hate content, or disruptive use.
  • Bandwidth fairness: Streaming may be limited; excessive use may be restricted to keep service reliable.
Prohibited examples: hacking attempts, malware distribution, piracy/torrenting, circumventing content filters, unauthorized hotspots/bridging, or interfering with district devices/services.
Privacy, Monitoring & Logging
  • This network is provided as-is and may be monitored for security, troubleshooting, and policy compliance.
  • Logs may include device identifiers, timestamps, IP addresses, and sites/domains accessed (depending on filtering tools).
  • There is no expectation of privacy when using district guest wireless.
Safety & Emergency Notice
  • Do not rely on Wi-Fi for emergency communications. For emergencies, dial 911 using cellular service or a landline.
  • Access may be interrupted without notice for maintenance, congestion, or security reasons.
Disclaimer & Liability
  • This is an unsecured guest network. Sensitive browsing is not recommended.
  • The district is not responsible for loss of data, device damage, malware, or any costs incurred from network use.
  • Access may be blocked or terminated at any time for any device violating policy or impacting performance.
